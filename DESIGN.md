
## Cover palette (2026-11-22)

The Devpost cover and the YouTube thumbnail (`scripts/cover.html`, `node scripts/cover-render.cjs [--yt out.jpg]`) use the "wave" palette, inspired by Katsushika Hokusai, *Under the Wave off Kanagawa* (c. 1830–32), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access (public domain): https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434. Deep Prussian blue `#0e1a2e`→`#142540`, wave blue `#2b4d7a`, foam cream `#f4ecdc`, gold `#ebbd52`.
